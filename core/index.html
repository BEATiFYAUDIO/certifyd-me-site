Creator-operated publishing

You run the publishing engine. People open your public link.

With a hosted platform, another company runs the application that holds your publishing account. With a Core installation you operate, your computer holds the work records and local media used by that installation. You manage the private dashboard and explicitly enable the separate public service.

  1. Prepare in CoreAdd the work, details and credits in your private dashboard.
  2. Enable public sharingUse a temporary public link or configure a permanent address on your domain.
  3. Share and be discoveredVisitors open your public page. Eligible discoverable work can also appear in Fan.

Where does Certifyd Fan fit?

Certifyd Fan is the discovery and playback experience. It brings together eligible public work from configured creator Core origins. Your Core supplies the work, offers and access decisions; Fan helps people browse and reach them. Public sharing does not guarantee inclusion, ranking or an audience.

Your computer and public connection need to stay online to serve local work. A domain, tunnel or payment provider may still be part of your setup. Core is not an automatic backup or a promise that another node will host your files.

Context and commerce

Keep the credits attached. Set up commerce when you need it.

Who is connected to the work?

Core supports creator profiles, account-link proofs, work credits and versioned participant splits. Supported public views can show the people and recorded relationships behind a work.

An account proof connects an account to a creator profile. It does not establish legal identity or authorship of every upload. Recorded attribution and split percentages are not, by themselves, proof of ownership or a payout.

Can I sell directly to fans?

Core beta supports free publication and paid-access workflows using supported Bitcoin and Lightning commerce. Paid publishing requires the applicable Sovereign setup, a permanent public identity/address and a ready payment-provider or node connection.

A Lightning Address for support is separate from the setup needed to sell paid work. Purchase access, accounting allocations and actual payouts are different stages; earnings and payout timing are not guaranteed.

Start with the basics

What do I need to run Core?

  • A supported computer. Choose the Windows, macOS or Linux package that matches your operating system and architecture on the downloads page.
  • Storage and backups. Allow space for your work and Core’s local data. Keep backups and the recovery material required by your account and creator-key setup.
  • An internet connection for public access. Core and its public connection must be running when visitors request locally served work.
  • A sharing setup. Begin with the guide’s Basic path for a temporary link and free solo work. Continue to Sovereign Creator for a domain and supported paid works, or Sovereign Node to operate payment services.

Install Core, create the first owner account, set up your profile and add a work. Publication, public sharing and payment readiness are separate steps. Follow the guide rather than exposing the private dashboard to the internet.
