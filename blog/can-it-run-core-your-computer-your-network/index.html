Can It Run Core? Your Computer. Your Network.

Four computers. One question. Watch Certifyd Core turn creator-owned infrastructure into a public catalog, discovery, verified identity and a real Lightning purchase.

By CertifydPublished November 10, 2026Updated November 10, 2026
creator infrastructurecreator ownershipdirect-to-fanBitcoin LightningCertifyd Core

How much of your creator business could you run on a computer you already own?

That was the question behind our latest Certifyd commercial. We started with the computer already running my Core, then a mini PC, a laptop and a Chromebook. A small hardware parade with one very practical question: can it run Core?

The answer matters because the computer on your desk can do more than make content. It can run the infrastructure behind your identity, your catalog, your publishing and your relationship with fans.

So we made a video showing the pieces working together. Real founder footage. Real software. A real purchase and unlock. Two minutes to follow a work from the creator's computer into a fan's hands.

Your Computer Gets Another Job

Creators are used to making their work on their own machines, then handing the business end to somebody else.

Upload the music here. Put the videos there. Manage your profile somewhere else. Keep another tab open to work out what you earned.

Certifyd starts with the idea that creators should be able to operate that infrastructure themselves.

Certifyd Core is software you install on your computer. It gives you a local dashboard for your creator identity, work records, publishing and participation in the network. The small computer in the opening is already doing that job.

The commercial follows a genuine Windows installation of Core Beta 27 through download, setup, first launch and the working dashboard. Published builds also cover macOS and Linux. The Chromebook shown uses a compatible Linux environment; there is no separate native ChromeOS installer. Hardware and operating-system compatibility still matter, so choose the package for your machine on the downloads page.

The interesting question is what you do once Core is running.

Works, Library, Profile, Revenue, Audience

The demonstration gives the software room to speak for itself. You can see the navigation, the screens and how the different parts belong together.

Works: Author Anything Digital

Works is where you manage your catalog: the work record, its files, descriptive information and publication details. Music can sit alongside software, video, podcasts, photography or documents.

You decide what you want to share. Your catalog begins in infrastructure you operate, with records connecting your identity to your work. Those records support attribution and provenance; they do not, by themselves, establish legal copyright ownership.

Library: Keep Your Work in View

Library gives you another view of the works in your Core environment. The point is practical: your creative records belong in the software you run, where you can find and manage them.

Profile: Give Fans Somewhere to Find You

Your public profile brings your identity and work together. You can connect supported social accounts with verification proofs, giving people a way to check the relationship between a Certifyd identity and the accounts they already know.

A recognizable public presence helps fans follow the work back to its creator.

Revenue: See the Business Side

The revenue snapshot shows the commerce side of Core. Bitcoin and Lightning payments are available through supported configurations, with the actual payment and payout behavior depending on the services connected to the creator's setup.

Audience: Understand Your Reach

The audience map makes network activity visible. It is a view of reach and access activity, not a claim that every request represents a unique fan or a sale.

Together, these screens show the idea behind Core: creators should have useful tools for operating their own creative business.

The Moment It Becomes Real

The central reveal is simple.

First, you see a work being managed inside Core. You see that it is published and has a public link. Then you see that same work accessible online.

The creator's computer and the fan's experience are connected.

The recording follows an existing published work. It shows its real status and public destination; it does not stage a new Publish action for the camera.

That connection is the heart of Certifyd. Core is where the creator operates. Public sharing makes the work reachable. Discovery gives fans a way to find it.

For public access, Core and the configured public connection need to be available. Owning the infrastructure also means operating it.

A Purchase You Can Watch Happen

The handheld payment footage stays with the actual experience: a purchase, payment confirmation and access to the work unlocking.

You can see why we kept it candid. A phone, a screen, a little waiting, then the result. That's the part you want to see when somebody says the system works.

The demonstration shows a real Bitcoin Lightning purchase and unlock, with a payment receipt on the phone. It is evidence of that transaction, rather than a promise about every wallet or every participant's settlement. Payment routing and payouts follow the configuration in use.

The larger idea is straightforward: fans should be able to support creators, and creators should be able to sell work from infrastructure they control.

Turn on Public Discovery

Now follow the story into Certifyd Discovery, available at fan.certifyd.me.

Fans can find music, software, videos, podcasts, photography and other digital work. The commercial moves through real creator pages and artwork so you can see the variety for yourself.

You will encounter work from AnSolas, Agent13, Bitcoin Sherpa, Stillwaters and Beatify, alongside Blessedrthe's public work in the publishing and purchase sequence. Different creators, different kinds of work, connected through the same network.

Certifyd is a network. Core, Discovery and Browser play different roles within it. Creators operate their own infrastructure; fans can discover and interact with the work through public interfaces.

No custodial platform needs to hold the entire catalog just to make that relationship possible.

Customize Your Socials

Creators already have communities on other websites. Those relationships matter.

Certifyd Browser brings verified creator context to supported sites people already use. In the commercial, you see a real account verification, then a creator card and a support option on an existing social page.

The Alby example opens a support request. The completed purchase and unlock are the earlier transaction; this later example does not show a second completed payment.

This is how Certifyd can fit into a creator's existing online life. Keep using your favourite supported sites while giving people a way to verify your identity and connect with your work.
