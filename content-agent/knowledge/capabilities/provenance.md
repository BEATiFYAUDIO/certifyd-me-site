# Provenance

## Definition

Provenance is evidence about the origin, source, publisher, timestamp, contribution context, release history, or record history of a work. It is not automatically legal proof of authorship, ownership, or rights.

## Why It Exists

Provenance exists to make creative context clearer and more portable as work moves across profiles, discovery, commerce, recognition, and partner surfaces.

## Architectural Role

Provenance is included in the approved Core definition, but existing knowledge marks provenance and proof-backed language as needing stronger definition and technical evidence.
