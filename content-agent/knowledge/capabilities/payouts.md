# Payouts

## Definition

Payouts are the movement or distribution of earnings to creators or participants after payment or allocation. Payments received and payouts distributed are separate events.

## Why It Exists

Payouts exist so creator commerce and split contexts can result in participants receiving allocated value.

## Architectural Role

Payouts are mentioned in public knowledge but implementation and guarantees are unclear.
