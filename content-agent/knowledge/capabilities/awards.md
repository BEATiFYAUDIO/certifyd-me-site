# Awards

## Definition

Awards is the recognition capability and public product surface for creators, works, partners, and infrastructure activity. Nominations, verification, scoring, fan participation, voting, payments, rewards, Network data, and proof-backed ranking are separate capabilities.

## Why It Exists

Awards exists to recognize creative, cultural, technical, and ecosystem contribution while connecting recognition to the broader Certifyd system where supported.

## Architectural Role

Awards is a public ecosystem surface powered by or connected to the broader Certifyd system. Current public Awards should be separated from planned proof, scoring, rewards, commerce, fan, and Network integrations.
