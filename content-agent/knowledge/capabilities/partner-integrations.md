# Partner Integrations

## Definition

Partner integrations are relationships or technical connections with creators, operators, businesses, developers, agencies, labels, institutions, or service providers that extend Certifyd capabilities.

## Why It Exists

Partner integrations exist so Certifyd can support more creators and commerce use cases without every function being operated centrally.

## Architectural Role

Investor knowledge describes services, integrations, provider participation, enterprise pilots, and partner programs as planned or funding-dependent.
