# Analytics

## Definition

Analytics is the capability for measuring and reporting activity. Public activity, sales data, playback data, catalog performance, audience insights, and financial reporting are separate categories.

## Why It Exists

Analytics exists so creators, partners, and Certifyd can understand activity and business performance, subject to privacy and data rights.

## Architectural Role

Investor materials describe analytics, AI, discovery, diagnostics, reporting, and catalog intelligence as funding-dependent or planned. Public knowledge does not verify comprehensive analytics.
