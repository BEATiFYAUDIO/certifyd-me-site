# Vocabulary

## Source Scope

This vocabulary applies the founder-approved product-architecture decision for Certifyd Core. It is for knowledge-base use only and does not modify public website copy.

## Product and Architecture Terms

### Certifyd

Certifyd is the overall brand and connected ecosystem. It includes the foundational Core engine, public product surfaces, network infrastructure, creator profiles, and commerce-oriented experiences.

### Certifyd Core

Certifyd Core is the foundational engine that powers identity, publishing, provenance, creator profiles, release records, access, and direct commerce across the Certifyd ecosystem.

**Status rule:** This is an architectural definition. Individual Core capabilities must still be described separately as `LIVE`, `BETA`, `PLANNED`, `FUNDING-DEPENDENT`, `TRANSACTION-DEPENDENT`, or `UNCLEAR`.

### Certifyd Network

Certifyd Network is the distributed infrastructure layer through which creators, partners, and operators can run, extend, host, distribute, or connect Certifyd capabilities.

**Open technical question:** The exact runtime relationship between Certifyd Core, creator/operator nodes, and Certifyd Network still requires technical confirmation.

### Certifyd Fan

Certifyd Fan is the fan-facing app or experience for discovering, accessing, playing, collecting, and directly supporting creator work. It is powered by or connected to Certifyd Core.

### Certifyd Awards

Certifyd Awards is the recognition layer for creators, works, partners, and infrastructure activity. It is a public ecosystem surface that can use records, fan activity, commerce, and Network participation from the broader Certifyd system where those integrations are live or supported.

### Creator Profiles

Creator Profiles are public creator-facing surfaces powered by Certifyd Core. They can present creator identity, work context, catalog context, publishing context, and relationship surfaces. They are not a separate top-level product in the approved hierarchy.

### Certifyd Creator

`Certifyd Creator` is legacy or inconsistent terminology unless a later founder decision restores it as a product name. Do not use it as a top-level product in new content.

## Status Terms

### LIVE

A capability or surface that is publicly demonstrated or currently available in a supported implementation.

### BETA

A capability or surface that exists but should be described as early, limited, or still being hardened.

### PLANNED

A capability or surface that may be discussed only as future direction, not as currently delivered.

### FUNDING-DEPENDENT

A capability or expansion that depends on future financing, staffing, infrastructure, or commercial execution.

### TRANSACTION-DEPENDENT

A capability, asset, or claim whose availability depends on completing a transaction, acquisition, consolidation, or ownership transfer.

### UNCLEAR

A capability or term that lacks enough approved evidence for public claim use.

## Writing Distinctions

### Architecture Versus Availability

Architecture explains where a capability belongs in the system. Availability explains whether that capability is live, beta, planned, funding-dependent, transaction-dependent, or unclear. Do not use the approved Core architecture to imply all Core capabilities are live.

### Product Versus Surface

Fan and Awards are public product surfaces. Creator Profiles are Core-powered public surfaces. Core is the foundational engine. Network is the distributed infrastructure layer.

### Ecosystem Versus Company

Use `Certifyd` for the public brand and ecosystem. Use more precise legal or corporate language only when discussing investment, ownership, contracts, or governance.
