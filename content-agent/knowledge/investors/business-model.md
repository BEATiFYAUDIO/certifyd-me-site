# Business Model

## Source Scope

This file extracts business-model claims from the investor site, especially the Investment page sections titled The Opportunity, The Model, Why Certifyd Wins and What The Investment Unlocks.

## Business Model Summary

Certifyd is positioned to earn from creator-business activity rather than from owning creators or relying only on advertising attention.

- **Classification:** `VISION` / `PROJECTED`.
- **Source:** Investment page — Hero; Investment page — The Opportunity; Investment page — The Model.

## Core Model

One creator can connect to multiple revenue streams through Certifyd-connected products and services.

- **Classification:** `VISION` / `PROJECTED`.
- **Source:** Investment page — The Model.
- **Business meaning:** Certifyd's upside is framed as expanding monetization surfaces around each creator relationship.

## Customer and Participant Groups

### Creators

Creators are the primary economic subject: they publish, sell, receive support, build customer relationships and participate in public profile/discovery surfaces.

- **Classification:** `BETA` / `VISION` depending on exact capability.
- **Source:** Investor homepage — Hero; Investment page — The Opportunity.

### Fans / Customers

Fans discover, follow, support and buy from creators through Certifyd-connected products.

- **Classification:** `LIVE` for some public discovery surfaces; `BETA` / `PLANNED` for full commerce breadth.
- **Source:** Investment page — The Model; existing `knowledge/vocabulary.md`.

### Partners / Operators

Partners and operators are presented as participants who can operate parts of the system and expand capacity.

- **Classification:** `BETA` / `PLANNED`.
- **Source:** Investor homepage — Hero; Investment page — Why Certifyd Wins.
- **Risk:** Existing approved claims mark node/operator definitions as needing founder decision.

### Enterprises / Institutions

Enterprises, institutions and managed service customers appear in the use-of-funds and execution plan.

- **Classification:** `FUNDING-DEPENDENT` / `PLANNED`.
- **Source:** Investment page — What The Investment Unlocks; Investment page — Execution Plan.

## Business Advantages

### Creator-Owned Business Positioning

Certifyd helps creators run more of their own business rather than renting platform access.

- **Classification:** `VISION`.
- **Source:** Investment page — Hero.
- **Limit:** Avoid legal or absolute ownership guarantees.

### Shared Operating Model

As more creators and partners join, Certifyd aims to share more of the operating model instead of centralizing every cost.

- **Classification:** `VISION` / `BETA`.
- **Source:** Investment page — The Opportunity; Investment page — Why Certifyd Wins.

### Multiple Product Surfaces

Fan, Awards, profiles, commerce and future enterprise products are positioned as surfaces using the same foundation.

- **Classification:** `LIVE` for some public surfaces; `PROJECTED` for broad cross-product economics.
- **Source:** Investor homepage — Live products; Investment page — Why Certifyd Wins.

### Commerce-Based Activity

Certifyd emphasizes commerce as more meaningful than views and clicks.

- **Classification:** `VISION`.
- **Source:** Investment page — Why Certifyd Wins.
- **Limit:** Do not convert this into fraud-proof or guaranteed-authenticity language.

## Funding-Dependent Business Buildout

The investor page states that funding would build:

- Product improvements.
- Commercial organization.
- Creator and partner onboarding.
- Enterprise packaging.
- Security, QA, monitoring and operations.

- **Classification:** `FUNDING-DEPENDENT`.
- **Source:** Investment page — What The Investment Unlocks.
