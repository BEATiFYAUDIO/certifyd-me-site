# Revenue Model

## Source Scope

This file extracts revenue model claims from the public investor site, primarily the Investment page section titled The Model.

## Revenue Thesis

Certifyd connects businesses around creators into one economy. Every service is presented as creating value for creators while creating another opportunity for Certifyd to earn.

- **Classification:** `VISION` / `PROJECTED`.
- **Source:** Investment page — The Model.

## Payment-Rail Claim

Bitcoin and Lightning are presented as a neutral settlement base today, with other payment rails possible over time.

- **Classification:** `BETA` for supported Lightning use; `PLANNED` for broader rails.
- **Source:** Investment page — The Model.
- **Existing-knowledge comparison:** Existing approved claims allow `Bitcoin Lightning payments where available`, but discourage universal or guaranteed settlement claims.

## Revenue Model Risk Notes

- The investor site lists revenue categories but does not provide current revenue totals.
- Several revenue streams appear projected rather than proven.
- Subscriptions, registration fees, service fees and usage fees need current status confirmation.
- Commerce revenue needs proof of live transaction scope, payment coverage and entitlement behavior.
- Developer ecosystem revenue is likely roadmap unless developer products are already public.

## Safe Writing Guidance

- Say Certifyd is designed for multiple revenue streams.
- Do not say every listed stream is currently generating revenue unless verified.
- Keep Bitcoin/Lightning language to `where supported` or `settlement base today` with careful limits.
- Treat enterprise, services and developer revenue as planned or funding-dependent unless contracts exist.
