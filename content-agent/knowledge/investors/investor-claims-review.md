# Investor Claims Review

## Source Scope

This review compares claims from the public investor site with existing extracted public-site knowledge and `approved-public-claims.md`.

## Claim Classification Key

- `LIVE`: Publicly inspectable or stated as currently available.
- `BETA`: Early or limited current capability.
- `PLANNED`: Intended future capability.
- `FUNDING-DEPENDENT`: Dependent on the proposed investment.
- `TRANSACTION-DEPENDENT`: Dependent on the proposed transaction or legal structure.
- `PROJECTED`: Forward-looking milestone, revenue, or expansion claim.
- `VISION`: Strategic direction or positioning.
- `UNCLEAR`: Insufficient support or inconsistent source material.

## Claims That Conflict With Or Extend Existing Knowledge

1. Investor site defines `Certifyd Core`; existing approved public claims mark Core as needing founder decision.
2. Investor site uses stronger creator-owned language; existing knowledge recommends dependency-reduction wording unless ownership scope is defined.
3. Investor site frames public products as part of one commercial system; existing knowledge supports links but not all integration claims.
4. Investor site presents multiple revenue streams; existing public knowledge does not verify current revenue by stream.
5. Investor site references AI economics; existing public-site knowledge does not establish AI as a current product capability.

## Claims To Avoid Until Verified

- Certifyd has already reached commercial scale.
- Certifyd has active revenue in all six revenue streams.
- Certifyd Core is fully defined and generally available.
- Certifyd Network is decentralized or company-independent.
- Creators legally own all customer relationships through Certifyd.
- Commerce activity is fraud-proof.
- Enterprise and white-label deployments are already live.
- The $20M transaction is closed, committed, or legally finalized.

## Founder Decisions Required

1. Whether the investor-site definition of Certifyd Core is now authoritative.
2. Which Core capabilities are live, beta, planned, or funding-dependent.
3. Whether identity and commerce belong under Core, Network, or both.
4. Whether Certifyd Network should be publicly defined as discovery, routing and distribution only.
5. Which revenue streams are currently live.
6. Whether `creator-owned infrastructure` is approved public language.
7. Whether `creators own the customer` should be softened for public/legal safety.
8. Which investment-use allocations are final enough to publish.
9. Whether the $20M structure should be described as investment, acquisition, or transaction.
