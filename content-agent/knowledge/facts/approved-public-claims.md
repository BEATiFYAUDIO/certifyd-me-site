# Approved Public Claims

## Product Hierarchy

### Certifyd

The overall brand and ecosystem for creator publishing, discovery, ownership, direct commerce, profiles, network participation, and recognition surfaces.

- **Live/publicly demonstrated:** The public website, Join Network path, Register Node page, Certifyd Fan link, Certifyd Awards link, and a live public Certifyd profile link are present in the source material.
- **Beta/early:** The source material says Certifyd launched a technical beta in 2026 and that the network is early.
- **Planned/directional:** Creator-owned publishing, discovery, and direct commerce across the open web are described as the direction Certifyd is working toward.

### Certifyd Core

Certifyd Core is the foundational engine that powers identity, publishing, provenance, creator profiles, release records, access, and direct commerce across the Certifyd ecosystem.

- **Architecture:** Approved. Core is the foundational engine at the centre of the Certifyd ecosystem.
- **Qualification:** Individual Core capabilities may be live, in beta, planned, funding-dependent, transaction-dependent, or available only in particular implementations. Public content must not imply that every Core capability is generally available today.
- **Implementation status:** Capability-specific. The architectural definition does not prove that every listed capability is live.
- **Commercial status:** Separate from architecture. Core being architectural foundation does not prove current revenue for every Core-powered capability.

### Certifyd Network

The distributed infrastructure layer through which creators, partners, and operators can run, extend, host, distribute, or connect Certifyd capabilities.

The exact operational relationship between Core and individual nodes may still require technical confirmation. Do not describe Certifyd Network as fully decentralized, company-independent, permanent, immutable, or uncontrolled by one company without verified architecture and governance evidence.

### Certifyd Fan

The fan-facing application and experience for discovering, accessing, playing, collecting, and directly supporting creator work. Fan is powered by or connected to Certifyd Core.

- **Live/publicly demonstrated:** The homepage links to `https://fan.certifyd.me` as `Discover`.
- **Potential functionality from source:** Discovery, browsing creator profiles, unlocking premium releases, playback, collection, and supporting creators are described in source material.
- **Limit:** Specific Fan functionality should be verified in the Fan product before being described as live.

### Certifyd Awards

The recognition layer for creators, works, partners, and infrastructure activity within the Certifyd ecosystem. Awards is a public ecosystem surface that can use proof, records, fan activity, commerce, and Network participation supplied through the broader Certifyd system.

- **Live/publicly demonstrated:** The homepage links to `https://awards.certifyd.me` and `https://awards.certifyd.me/nominate`.
- **Current website/program:** The source supports Awards as a recognition and nomination surface.
- **Future integrations:** Planned voting, scoring, rewards, commerce, fan activity, partner activity, and Network integrations must remain separate from current Awards capabilities.

### Creator Profiles

Creator profiles are public creator-facing surfaces powered by Certifyd Core. They are not currently a separate top-level product.

- **Live/publicly demonstrated:** Public profile surfaces are supported by source material.
- **Limit:** Full profile capability scope and rollout status still require confirmation.

### Certifyd Creator Naming

Treat `Certifyd Creator` as legacy or inconsistent terminology unless a later founder decision explicitly restores it as a product name.

Do not use `Certifyd Creator` as a top-level product in new content.

## Writing Rules

- Never omit Certifyd Core when explaining the ecosystem architecture.
- Never describe Core as merely a node application.
- Never use the architectural definition as evidence that every capability is live.
- Always distinguish the role of Core from the deployment status of its individual capabilities.
- Certifyd Core is the engine at the centre of the ecosystem.
- Never omit Core when explaining the product architecture.
- Never describe planned features as live.
- Always identify which product or layer performs a capability.
- Prefer `creator-controlled` or `reduces platform dependency` over absolute ownership claims unless stronger wording is supported.
- Distinguish Certifyd the brand from Certifyd Core and Certifyd Network.
- Do not use `platform` as the preferred word for Certifyd where `network`, `infrastructure`, `system`, or `ecosystem` is more accurate.
- Avoid legal guarantees.
- Avoid technical guarantees unless verified.
- Avoid permanent or immutable language unless technically and legally verified.
- Do not imply that Certifyd Core, Fan, Network, or Awards are interchangeable names.
- Do not use `Certifyd Creator` as a top-level product name unless restored by a later founder decision.
- Use `where supported` when describing Bitcoin Lightning payment settlement.
- Treat chatbot-derived claims as provisional until founder-approved.
