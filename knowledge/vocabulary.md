# Vocabulary

## Source Scope

This file captures recurring terms from the current Certifyd website in this repository only.

## Product and Brand Terms

### Certifyd

- Used as the main public brand.
- Described as creator commerce infrastructure and a system for publishing, commerce, receipts, payouts, and creator ownership.

### Certifyd Creator

- Used in the logo alt text and homepage metadata.
- Not separately defined on the page.

### Certifyd Network

- Used for the network concept and the node registration page.
- Described as a distributed creator commerce network connecting creators, fans, and operators through publishing, payments, and ownership infrastructure.
- Also described as infrastructure for creator commerce.

### The Independent Creator Network

- Used as a homepage subheading.
- Described as a growing network for creators, labels, and communities building profiles, selling directly, and owning audience relationships.

### Certifyd Fan

- Linked from the homepage as `Discover` at `https://fan.certifyd.me`.
- The chatbot says fans can browse creator profiles, discover content, unlock premium releases, and support creators directly.

### Certifyd Awards

- A public product surface at `https://awards.certifyd.me`.
- Described as celebrating outstanding creators, remarkable content, and contributors across creative and technical roles.
- Described as a two-day annual celebration of Creative Excellence and Creator Innovation.

## Capability Terms

### Creator Ownership

- Means creators maintain greater control over content, audience relationships, commerce, and earnings rather than depending entirely on centralized platforms.
- The Insights draft cautions that creator ownership is not only about owning a file.

### Creator Commerce

- Defined in chatbot copy as creators selling directly to their audience instead of relying only on advertising, streams, or platform-controlled monetization.

### Creator Profile

- Described as a storefront where fans can discover content, make purchases, and interact directly with creators.
- Also described as a source of truth for a creator.

### Source of Truth

- Used to describe a Certifyd profile relative to social profiles.
- Meaning: social profiles become channels; the Certifyd profile becomes the official place for identity and work context.

### Receipts

- Described as permanent records of purchases, payments, and content access.
- Said to provide transparency for creators and buyers.

### Splits

- Described as a way for multiple contributors to share revenue from a sale.
- The chatbot says Certifyd can track how earnings should be allocated among collaborators.

### Payouts

- Described as movement of earnings to creators and participants after revenue has been allocated.

### Royalties

- Used in homepage copy about managing royalties and growing publishing earnings.
- Not deeply defined on the public website.

### Bitcoin Lightning

- Described as a payment network built on Bitcoin that enables fast, low-cost payments.
- The homepage says a fan pays and the creator receives money directly over Bitcoin Lightning.
- The chatbot says Lightning can settle instantly where supported.

### Sovereign Node

- Used on the network page title and copy.
- Described as infrastructure already run by operators.
- Not fully defined for non-technical readers.

### Operator

- Used for people or organizations running infrastructure.
- The network page is explicitly for operators who already run infrastructure.

### Technical Beta

- The homepage says Certifyd launched technical beta in 2026.

## Inconsistent or Ambiguous Terms

- `Creator node`, `sovereign node`, `node`, and `infrastructure` appear related but are not consistently defined.
- `Discover`, `Fan`, and `creator discovery` are related but not clearly distinguished.
- `Profile`, `storefront`, and `source of truth` overlap.
- `Creator ownership`, `retain creator rights`, and `own your audience` are related but differ in legal and technical implications.
- `Distributed network` and `not controlled by a single platform or company` may need careful verification and wording.
