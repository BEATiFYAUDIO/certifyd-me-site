# Vision

## Source Scope

This file summarizes vision-oriented statements from the current Certifyd website in this repository only.

## Vision Summary

Certifyd describes a future where creators own their identity, audience relationships, commerce, and publishing context across the open web instead of depending entirely on centralized platforms.

## Current Capabilities

- The website currently presents a public Certifyd profile as a live production example.
- The website currently offers a Join Network path for creators, labels, builders, Bitcoiners, and operators.
- The website currently offers a Register Node page for existing infrastructure operators.
- The website currently links to Certifyd Fan and Certifyd Awards as public product surfaces.

## Planned or Directional Capabilities

- The network is described as early and in technical beta.
- The site seeks independent creators, labels, technologists, Bitcoiners, and operators to help build the network.
- The Insights draft frames creator-owned publishing, discovery, and direct commerce across the open web as the direction Certifyd is working toward.
- Future discovery systems are referenced in the Insights draft as systems that can benefit from cleaner publishing context.

## Vision Language from the Site

### Marketing Language

- `Own your identity.`
- `Own your audience.`
- `Own your commerce.`
- `Own your future.`
- `We’re building the alternative.`
- `A world where creators own their audience, control their commerce, publish independently, and participate in a distributed creator economy.`

### Philosophy

- Creator identity should not be limited to a single platform account.
- Audience relationships should survive platform changes.
- Commercial activity should connect to the source of the work.
- Public publishing context should make it easier to understand who published work, who contributed to it, and where the official record lives.
- Direct commerce should be closer to the creator.

## Inconsistent Wording Found

- The website says Certifyd helps creators `own your audience`, while the Insights draft uses more cautious language around reducing dependency and building customer relationships closer to an official presence.
- The chatbot describes a distributed network as `not controlled by a single platform or company`, which may be broader than the rest of the website currently proves.
- The site uses `sovereign`, `distributed`, `independent`, and `creator-owned` as related but not explicitly defined concepts.

## Open Questions

- Which parts of the vision are already live, which are beta, and which are future roadmap?
- Should `sovereign` remain part of the public vocabulary for mainstream creators?
- How should Certifyd define `distributed creator economy` in plain language?
- Should the website make a clearer distinction between open-web publishing, network infrastructure, and creator commerce?
