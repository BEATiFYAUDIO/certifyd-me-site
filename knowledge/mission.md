# Mission

## Source Scope

This file summarizes mission-related statements from the current Certifyd website in this repository only.

## Mission Summary

Certifyd exists to help creators, labels, and communities publish, sell directly, maintain stronger audience relationships, and reduce dependence on centralized platforms.

## Current Capabilities

- The homepage states that Certifyd is for creators, labels, and communities building profiles, selling directly, and owning the relationship with their audience.
- The site states that Certifyd helps creators publish, sell directly, route Lightning payments, manage royalties, track catalog performance, and grow earnings while retaining creator rights.
- The site presents a live Certifyd profile as evidence that Certifyd is in production.
- The site states that fans can purchase content directly.
- The chatbot copy says Certifyd combines publishing, commerce, receipts, payouts, and creator ownership into a single system.
- The chatbot copy says creators can publish music, videos, images, documents, digital products, downloads, memberships, premium content, and other digital media.

## Planned or Early Capabilities

- The website says Certifyd launched a technical beta in 2026.
- The website says the network is early.
- The website invites creators, labels, technologists, Bitcoiners, and operators to help build the network.
- The Insights draft says some surfaces are live today, while others are still being refined.

## Mission Language from the Site

### Marketing Language

- `The internet promised creators independence.`
- `Instead, most creators became dependent on platforms for discovery, distribution, payments, and audience access.`
- `Certifyd is for creators, labels, and communities who believe ownership still matters.`
- `The network is early. The mission is not.`
- `Direct creator ownership.`

### Philosophy

- Creators should maintain greater control over content, commerce, audience relationships, receipts, and creative rights.
- Creator businesses should be able to expand without rebuilding from zero on every platform.
- Social profiles can be channels, while a Certifyd profile can act as the source of truth.

## Inconsistent Wording Found

- The mission is described through several lenses: creator ownership, creator commerce, publishing, payments, distributed infrastructure, and fan discovery.
- The site uses both `creator commerce infrastructure` and `independent creator network`; these overlap but are not formally reconciled.
- The chatbot says creators retain ownership of receipts and audience relationships, which may need clearer product/legal definition.
