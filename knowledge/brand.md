# Brand

## Source Scope

This file summarizes public-facing brand information from the current Certifyd website in this repository only.

## Brand Names and Surfaces

### Current Capabilities

- The public website presents the brand as `Certifyd`.
- The logo is used with the accessible label `Certifyd Creator logo` on the homepage and network page.
- The homepage title is `Certifyd | Own Your Content and Revenue`.
- The network page title is `Register a Sovereign Node | Certifyd Network`.
- The website links to these public surfaces:
  - `https://fan.certifyd.me` for discovery.
  - `https://public.certifyd.me/u/certifyd` as a live Certifyd profile.
  - `https://awards.certifyd.me` for Certifyd Awards.
  - `https://awards.certifyd.me/nominate` for nominations.
  - `https://network.certifyd.me/join` for node registration.

### Planned Capabilities

- The website presents the network as early and in technical beta.
- The Insights draft says some surfaces are live while others are still being refined.

## Core Brand Language

### Marketing Language

- `Own Your Audience`
- `For people building something worth owning.`
- `The Independent Creator Network`
- `Build The Alternative`
- `Own your identity. Own your audience. Own your commerce. We’re building the alternative.`
- `Take your audience. Take your income.`
- `Create → Publish` and `Sell → Get Paid`
- `See Certifyd in production`
- `Your social profiles become channels. Your Certifyd profile becomes the source of truth.`

### Brand Positioning

- Certifyd positions itself around creator ownership, direct commerce, publishing, identity, audience relationships, and payments.
- The homepage frames Certifyd as an alternative to creator dependence on platforms for discovery, distribution, payments, and audience access.
- The brand emphasizes ownership rather than platform dependency.
- Certifyd Awards extends the brand into creator recognition, culture, credits, and annual celebration.

## Visual and Tone Notes

### Current Capabilities

- The public website uses a dark visual style with blue, orange, and high-contrast creator imagery.
- The homepage uses creator, payment, IP/publishing, and awards imagery.
- The site uses direct CTA language such as `Discover`, `Join Network`, `Register Node`, and `Explore the Awards`.

## Inconsistent Wording Found

- The site alternates between `Certifyd`, `Certifyd Creator`, `Certifyd Network`, and `The Independent Creator Network` without explicitly defining their relationship.
- The homepage title says `Own Your Content and Revenue`, while the hero says `Own Your Audience`.
- The brand is sometimes described as creator commerce infrastructure, sometimes as a creator network, sometimes as a distributed creator commerce network, and sometimes as a profile/source-of-truth system.
- The site uses both `Join Network` and `Register Node`; the distinction is present but could be clearer.

## Open Questions

- Should the primary public brand be `Certifyd` or `Certifyd Creator`?
- Is `The Independent Creator Network` an official product name, a tagline, or a descriptive phrase?
- Should `Own Your Audience` remain the main homepage headline, or should the broader `Own Your Content and Revenue` positioning be unified with it?
- Should `Certifyd Network` be presented as a separate product surface or the underlying infrastructure layer?
