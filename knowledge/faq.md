# FAQ

## Source Scope

This file captures FAQ-style information from the current Certifyd website, including visible page copy and the homepage chatbot responses.

## Current Capabilities

### What is Certifyd?

Certifyd is described as creator commerce infrastructure that helps creators publish content, accept payments, manage ownership, and connect directly with their audience.

### Is Certifyd only for music distribution?

No. The chatbot says Certifyd supports music distribution but is broader than traditional distribution. It also supports publishing, selling, payments, rights, and direct audience relationships.

### What can creators publish?

The chatbot says creators can publish music, videos, images, documents, digital products, downloads, memberships, premium content, and other digital media.

### Do creators keep ownership of their content?

The chatbot says creators retain ownership of their content, audience relationships, receipts, and creative rights. This should be clarified later because the exact legal and technical meaning is not defined on the site.

### How do creators get paid?

The site says fans can purchase content directly. Payments are processed through the Certifyd commerce system and can settle instantly using Bitcoin Lightning where supported.

### What is Bitcoin Lightning?

The chatbot describes Bitcoin Lightning as a payment network built on Bitcoin that enables fast, low-cost payments.

### Do users need to understand Bitcoin?

The chatbot says not necessarily. Certifyd is designed to make creator commerce simple while supporting Bitcoin-powered payments behind the scenes where available.

### How is Certifyd different from other platforms?

The chatbot says other platforms primarily control distribution and audience access, while Certifyd focuses on creator ownership, direct commerce, transparent payouts, and audience relationships.

### What is creator commerce?

Creator commerce means creators can sell directly to their audience instead of relying only on advertising, streams, or platform-controlled monetization.

### What are receipts?

Receipts are described as permanent records of purchases, payments, and content access that provide transparency for creators and buyers.

### What are splits?

Splits allow multiple contributors to share revenue from a sale. The chatbot says Certifyd can track how earnings should be allocated among collaborators.

### What are payouts?

Payouts are the movement of earnings to creators and participants after revenue has been allocated.

### Can creators collaborate?

The chatbot says yes. Certifyd supports collaboration through revenue sharing, contributor participation, attribution, and payout tracking.

### What is a Certifyd profile?

A Certifyd profile is described as a creator storefront where fans can discover content, make purchases, and interact directly with creators. The homepage also describes it as a source of truth.

### Can fans discover creators?

The chatbot says fans can browse creator profiles, discover content, unlock premium releases, and support creators directly.

### What is the Certifyd Network?

The chatbot describes it as a distributed creator commerce network that connects creators, fans, and operators through publishing, payments, and ownership infrastructure.

### Who is Certifyd for?

The chatbot says Certifyd is designed for musicians, creators, labels, publishers, educators, media companies, creator collectives, and independent operators.

### Why does Bitcoin matter?

The chatbot says Bitcoin enables borderless payments, global accessibility, and direct value transfer without requiring traditional banking infrastructure.

### How do people join?

The homepage says to use the Join Network form. The network page directs existing operators to `https://network.certifyd.me/join`.

## Planned or Early Capabilities

- The network is in technical beta.
- Some creator-owned publishing, discovery, and direct commerce surfaces are still being refined.
- The site is recruiting independent creators, labels, technologists, Bitcoiners, and operators.

## Unclear Product Claims

- `Creators retain ownership of their audience relationships, receipts, and creative rights` needs clearer legal/product definition.
- `Receipts are permanent records` needs clarification about where permanence comes from and what guarantees exist.
- `Distributed network is not controlled by a single platform or company` needs careful validation against current architecture and governance.
- `Fans can unlock premium releases` appears in chatbot copy and should be confirmed against current public product availability.
- `Manage royalties` and `track catalog performance` appear on the homepage but are not explained in detail.
