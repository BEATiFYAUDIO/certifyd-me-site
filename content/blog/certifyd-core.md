Certifyd Core is a central component of the platform designed to support creators, creators, partners, and businesses. It provides a public and official way for creators to present their work, identity, and context. The Core is a publicly approved and live public surface powered by Certifyd, allowing creators to share their work and identity in a transparent and verified manner. The Core is designed to support a wide range of product surfaces, including Creator Profiles, Fan, Network, and Partner Infrastructure, among others. The Core is intended to be a living and evolving part of the platform, with ongoing development and improvements as needed. The Core is built on a foundation of provenance, receipts, and other capabilities that are currently under development and verification. The Core is intended to support creators and businesses in a transparent and verified way, with ongoing improvements and enhancements as needed.
