Some artists need an introduction. B.B. King needs only a note.

That voice. That touch. Lucille answering every phrase. The sound of a musician who could make a room lean forward with a single bend of a guitar string.

This November, there is more to hear.

**B.B. King: The Montreux Years arrives November 20, 2026, via BMG**, bringing together rare live performances from one of the great relationships in music: B.B. King and the Montreux Jazz Festival.

The first taste is already here. **“Early in the Mornin’,” recorded live at Montreux in 2000, is available now.**

## Turn it up: the first single is here

“Early in the Mornin’” finds King paying tribute to one of his musical heroes, **Louis Jordan**. Recorded at Auditorium Stravinski in 2000, the performance offers an immediate invitation into the collection, with an accompanying video drawn from that festival appearance.

For longtime fans, it is another chance to hear a familiar master at work. For anyone discovering King for the first time, it is a good place to start: with the music playing.

## A legendary artist. A legendary stage.

King appeared at Montreux **23 times**, returning at the invitation of festival founder and friend **Claude Nobs**. Those appearances became part of the festival’s history, and this collection opens a window onto performances recorded between **1982 and 2000**.

The songs include “The Thrill Is Gone,” “Caldonia,” “Rock Me Baby” and “Guess Who.” Familiar titles, heard through particular nights onstage: the phrasing, the band, the room and the moment.

There is plenty for collectors to anticipate, too. **All but one of the recordings are previously unreleased.** The double CD and HD digital editions draw from nine concerts; the double vinyl edition features performances from eight.

The recordings have been restored and newly mastered from the original tapes by **Tony Cousins at Metropolis Studios in London**. Rare and previously unseen photography and specially commissioned liner notes by B.B. King biographer **Daniel de Visé** complete the package.

## Music worth hearing again—and discovering for the first time

Behind these performances is the extraordinary archive assembled by Claude Nobs. The Montreux collection was inscribed on **UNESCO’s Memory of the World Register in 2013**, recognising its importance as documentary heritage.

For listeners, the reward is wonderfully direct: press play, hear King, and spend some time with music that still has somewhere to take you.

A great live recording can bring a distant stage close. It lets an old favourite surprise you, gives a new listener a way in, and keeps a performance moving from one generation to the next.

## Vassal Benford and the B.B. King legacy

**Vassal Benford, executor of the B.B. King Estate,** is part of the ongoing story of King’s legacy. Created in close collaboration with the estate and the Montreux Jazz Festival team, this release gives fans another opportunity to celebrate the artist through his music.
