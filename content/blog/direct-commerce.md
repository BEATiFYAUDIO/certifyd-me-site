# Why Direct Creator Commerce Matters

Creators increasingly operate like small businesses. They sell access, releases, services, experiences and community participation across many places.

The more a creator sells directly, the more important it becomes to understand what was sold, who supported it and how that activity connects back to the creator's official presence.

## Discovery and commerce should connect

Discovery without commerce creates attention but not always business value. Commerce without discovery makes it harder for new audiences to find the creator.

Certifyd’s model connects public discovery, creator profiles and direct support so activity can point back to the creator instead of being trapped inside one destination platform.

## Direct does not mean isolated

Creator ownership does not require creators to operate alone. The useful model is a connected one: creators, fans, collaborators and operators can participate without forcing everything through one central platform account.
