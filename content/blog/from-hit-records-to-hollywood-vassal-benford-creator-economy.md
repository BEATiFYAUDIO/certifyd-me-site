# From Hit Records to Hollywood: Why Vassal Benford's Career Matters in Today's Creator Economy

### From the Recording Studio to the Boardroom

The modern entertainment business rewards creators who think beyond individual projects.

Few careers demonstrate that evolution better than producer, songwriter and entrepreneur **Vassal Benford**, whose work spans chart-topping music, film production, publishing and entertainment investment.

Recent projects continue to show the breadth of that career—and why ownership has become as important as creativity.

#### A Career That Continues to Produce

Benford's recent songwriting and production credits remain firmly rooted in today's commercial music landscape.

Jennifer Lopez's *This Is Me... Now* debuted at **No. 1 on Billboard's Top Album Sales Chart**, with Benford receiving composer and lyricist credits on **"Rebound,"** a collaboration featuring Latin artist Anuel AA.

His production work also appears on Jason Derulo's 2024 album *Nu King*, which brought together an unusually diverse collection of artists including Michael Bublé, Meghan Trainor, David Guetta, Quavo and Adam Levine.

Benford also earned songwriting credits on Chris Brown's *11:11 (Deluxe)*, including the tracks **"Bruce Lee"** and **"Delusional."** The album would go on to win **Best R&B Album** at the 2025 Grammy Awards, adding another milestone to a career that continues to span multiple genres and generations.

Rather than remaining confined to one musical style, Benford's work reflects a broader trend in today's entertainment industry: successful creators increasingly move across genres, formats and audiences while building long-term intellectual property.

#### Beyond Music

Benford's career has never been limited to records.

His early film work included original score contributions to films such as *New Jack City*, *House Party 2* and *Class Act*, demonstrating an ability to move between recorded music and visual storytelling.

That transition continued into executive production, including work on *After We Collided*, before expanding further into studio leadership.

Today, Benford serves as Co-Chairman and CEO of **Benford & Canton Studios**, alongside veteran producer Mark Canton. Among the studio's announced projects is a feature film based on the life of blues legend **B.B. King**—another example of intellectual property extending beyond its original medium.

#### The Evolution of the Modern Creator

Benford's career illustrates how the entertainment business has changed.

Today's creators are no longer simply musicians, filmmakers or producers.

They are increasingly becoming owners of brands, intellectual property, publishing rights, production companies and long-term creative businesses.

Success is no longer measured solely by chart positions or awards.

It is measured by the ability to create assets that continue generating value across music, film, publishing, licensing, merchandising and future creative works.

That shift—from creating content to owning creative infrastructure—is reshaping the entire creator economy.
