# SOCAN Acquires CMRRA from SoundExchange: A Strategic Move for Creators

### SOCAN Acquires CMRRA from SoundExchange: A Strategic Move for Creators

In a significant move that underscores the importance of transparency, accountability, and fair compensation in the music industry, SOCAN has acquired the Content Management Recordation and Reporting Authority (CMRRA) from SoundExchange. This acquisition is not just another business news article but a crucial step towards ensuring creators receive rightful recognition for their contributions.

#### The Importance of Provenance

Provenance is essential in creating trust within the music industry, where many creators often struggle with copyright disputes and fair compensation. By acquiring CMRRA, SOCAN aims to enhance its ability to provide accurate records of musical works, making it easier for creators to track ownership and ensure they are compensated fairly.
