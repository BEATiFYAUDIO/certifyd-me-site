## Your Computer Gets Another Job

Creators are used to making their work on their own machines, then handing the business end to somebody else.

Upload the music here. Put the videos there. Manage your profile somewhere else. Keep another tab open to work out what you earned.

Certifyd starts with the idea that creators should be able to operate that infrastructure themselves.

**Certifyd Core is software you install on your computer.** It gives you a local dashboard for your creator identity, work records, publishing and participation in the network. The small computer in the opening is already doing that job.

The commercial follows a genuine Windows installation of Core Beta 27 through download, setup, first launch and the working dashboard. Published builds also cover macOS and Linux. The Chromebook shown uses a compatible Linux environment; there is no separate native ChromeOS installer. ## Works, Library, Profile, Revenue, Audience

The demonstration gives the software room to speak for itself. You can see the navigation, the screens and how the different parts belong together.

### Works: Author Anything Digital

Works is where you manage your catalog: the work record, its files, descriptive information and publication details. Music can sit alongside software, video, podcasts, photography or documents.

You decide what you want to share. Your catalog begins in infrastructure you operate, with records connecting your identity to your work. Those records support attribution and provenance; they do not, by themselves, establish legal copyright ownership.

### Library: Keep Your Work in View

Library gives you another view of the works in your Core environment. The point is practical: your creative records belong in the software you run, where you can find and manage them.

### Profile: Give Fans Somewhere to Find You

Your public profile brings your identity and work together. You can connect supported social accounts with verification proofs, giving people a way to check the relationship between a Certifyd identity and the accounts they already know.

A recognizable public presence helps fans follow the work back to its creator.

### Revenue: See the Business Side

The revenue snapshot shows the commerce side of Core. Bitcoin and Lightning payments are available through supported configurations, with the actual payment and payout behavior depending on the services connected to the creator's setup.

### Audience: Understand Your Reach

The audience map makes network activity visible. It is a view of reach and access activity, not a claim that every request represents a unique fan or a sale.

Together, these screens show the idea behind Core: creators should have useful tools for operating their own creative business.
