Certifyd Browser Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026. This policy describes the Android Certifyd Browser app, package me.certifyd.ar, including its bundled Certifyd AR extension.

Information stored on your device

The browser stores tabs, browsing history, bookmarks, site cookies, downloads, saved credentials, settings and site permissions as needed for its features. These remain on your device unless you use a feature that sends them elsewhere, such as Firefox Account sync. Android's backup feature may copy some app preferences when enabled in your device settings.

Private browsing does not make you anonymous to websites, your network provider, or services you choose to use.

Network requests and third parties

Opening a website or using search sends requests to that website or your selected search provider. They may receive your IP address, requested URL and normal browser request information. Websites you visit may set cookies or use their own analytics and advertising. Their privacy practices apply; the browser's tracking-protection controls can limit some activity.

If you choose to sign in to a Firefox Account and enable sync, Mozilla's account and sync services process the account information and the data you select for sync. The supported categories are tabs, browsing history and saved passwords. You can use the browser without signing in or syncing.

Certifyd AR

The bundled Certifyd AR extension checks creator information on supported sites. To show attribution, it can request public registry and creator-profile data from GitHub-hosted registry pages, Certifyd services, or creator-operated Cores. These requests may include the supported page's URL, a public account handle, or public page details needed to resolve the creator.

When a creator connects and uses AR publishing, the extension keeps its signing key and drafts in local extension storage. The private signing key is generated as non-exportable. A creator-initiated Publish action sends the signed record and public key to Certifyd's AR publishing service; approved records are publicly accessible through the GitHub-backed registry. The publishing service may use the request's network address for abuse prevention and rate limiting.

Device permissions

Websites and browser features may request camera, microphone, location, media or file access, and notifications. Android and the browser provide controls for these permissions. The browser also supports opening downloads, including user-initiated APK installation, and checking installed apps when needed to open links or integrate with other apps. Download and background-playback services may run while those features are in use.

Diagnostics, retention and deletion

This Android release does not enable the app's Mozilla or Sentry crash-reporting service. Local browsing information and AR drafts can remain on the device until cleared or the app is removed, subject to any Android backup you enabled. Firefox Account data follows Mozilla's account and sync controls; data sent to websites, search providers, public registries, Certifyd services or creator Cores follows those recipients' retention practices. Published AR records may remain public after local data is removed.

You can clear browsing data in the browser, change or revoke site permissions in the browser or Android settings, and turn off Firefox sync in account settings. Removing the app deletes its local app data from the device; Android backups and copies held by third parties may need separate deletion. For data sent to a Certifyd-operated service, contact us at the address below. For Firefox Account data, use Mozilla's account controls; for websites and creator-operated Cores, contact those operators.
