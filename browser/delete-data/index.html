Request deletion of your data

Effective October 29, 2026. This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with Certifyd Browser (me.certifyd.ar). The browser does not require a Certifyd Browser account to browse the web.

How to make a deletion request

  1. Email hello@certifyd.me with the subject Certifyd Browser data deletion request.
  2. Describe the Certifyd-operated service or AR publishing activity involved, and provide sufficient information to locate the data. Do not send passwords, private keys, or sensitive documents.
  3. We will review the request, explain which records we control, and advise if additional ownership verification is required before deletion.

Email a deletion request

You can submit a request after uninstalling the browser. No login or app installation is required to send the email.

What data can be deleted?

  • Data on your device: Browser history, tabs, bookmarks, cookies, saved credentials, settings, and local AR drafts or keys can remain on your device until you clear the applicable browser data or uninstall the app. Certifyd cannot remotely clear that locally stored data for you. Android backups, if enabled, may retain copies under your device and backup settings.
  • Data held by Certifyd-operated services: You may request deletion of identifiable non-public data related to supported-page attribution, AR publishing or operational records, where such data is held and can lawfully be deleted. We will explain the outcome and any limits or required verification.
  • Firefox Account data: Optional Firefox Account sync is operated by Mozilla. Manage synced tabs, history, passwords, and any account deletion through Mozilla's account controls; a request to Certifyd cannot delete a Mozilla account.
  • Websites and creator-operated Cores: Sites visited through the browser, search providers, and independently operated creator Cores control their own data. Contact those operators for deletion of data they hold.

What may be kept, and for how long?

  • On-device browser data: Remains until you clear it or remove the app, subject to any Android backup copies. The browser does not set a separate server-side retention period for this local data.
  • Publicly published Certifyd AR records: Signed records published to the public GitHub-backed registry may remain publicly available after local browser data is deleted or a creator revokes publishing authorization. Public repository history or third-party copies may persist indefinitely and cannot be guaranteed removable.
  • Operational and abuse-prevention records: Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal or operational reasons. No universal fixed additional retention period applies to every service or operator; we will identify applicable limitations when processing a request.
  • Third-party data: Mozilla, Android backup services, public registry hosts, visited websites, and creator-operated Cores apply their own retention and deletion practices.

For more details about collection, sharing, and your choices, read the Certifyd Browser Privacy Policy.
