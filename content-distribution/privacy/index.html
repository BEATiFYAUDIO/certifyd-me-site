Certifyd Content Distribution Privacy Notice

Effective November 1, 2026. This notice concerns the Certifyd Content Distribution tool used by authorized Certifyd editors to publish Certifyd articles to connected outlets. It is separate from the Certifyd Browser and Certifyd AR privacy notices.

Google Blogger access

When an authorized editor chooses Connect Blogger, Google asks that person to authorize access to their Blogger account. The tool requests the Blogger permission shown on Google's consent screen. It uses that access to check which blogs the account can manage, verify the configured blog, and create or update posts on that blog after an editor initiates distribution. The tool does not use this access to delete Blogger posts.

Information we handle

We receive a Google authorization code and exchange it for access and refresh tokens. The refresh token is stored encrypted on the Certifyd dashboard server; short-lived access tokens are held in server memory. We also store connection status, the configured blog identifier, article content selected for publication, publishing results, destination post identifiers and URLs, and operational audit records. Published articles become visible on the chosen outlets according to their settings and policies.

Use and sharing

We use Blogger account access only to provide the requested Blogger connection and article-publishing functions. Article titles, text, images, labels, and attribution links selected for distribution are sent to Blogger when the editor publishes or updates a post. Google processes the authorization and Blogger posts under its own policies. We do not sell Google account data or use Blogger account access for advertising.

Retention and choices

The editor can disconnect Blogger in the dashboard, which removes the stored refresh token. The editor can also revoke Certifyd Content Distribution in their Google Account permissions. Published posts remain on Blogger until managed there; disconnecting does not delete them. Publishing records and operational audit information may remain to document what was sent and to help diagnose errors. Contact us to ask about personal information held in those records.

Security and contact

Dashboard access is restricted to authorized users. The stored Blogger refresh token is encrypted, and authorization responses are not written to public pages. If you have questions about this notice or a data request, email hello@certifyd.me.
