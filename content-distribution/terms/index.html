Certifyd Content Distribution Terms of Use

Effective November 1, 2026. Certifyd Content Distribution is an editorial tool for authorized Certifyd users to prepare and send Certifyd articles to connected publishing accounts.

Authorized use

Connect only accounts you are authorized to manage. Before publishing, check the article, images, links, destination selections, and any outlet-specific copy. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the rights to publish submitted material and that it complies with each destination's rules.

Connected services

Google Blogger and other connected outlets are independent services with their own terms and availability. Granting access allows the tool to perform the publishing actions you request. You can disconnect an outlet or revoke access through that service's account controls. Disconnecting does not remove content already published there.

Publishing results

Network failures, service limits, and account permissions can delay or prevent a post. Check each destination's result and public page before treating distribution as complete. A retry may be needed when an outlet reports a failed connection.

Privacy and contact

Our Content Distribution Privacy Notice explains how the tool handles connected-account information. Questions about this tool can be sent to hello@certifyd.me.
