Certifyd Content Distribution

Authorized Certifyd editors use this tool to review Certifyd articles and send approved content to connected publishing outlets. It is an internal editorial workflow, not a public account service.

How Blogger is used

An editor connects a Google account that manages the designated Blogger blog. The tool verifies access to that blog and, when the editor initiates distribution, creates or updates the selected article on Blogger. The editor can disconnect Blogger in the dashboard or revoke access in Google Account settings.
